# Client: Union Music

Current online presence: https://www.unionmusic.com/

## Audit
Problems with their current presence (at least 3):
1. The website has an older-looking design.
2. Some pages have a lot of information crowded together.
3. The navigation and layout could be easier to use.

## Audience and goal
Who their customers are:

Musicians, students, parents, and people looking to buy or repair musical instruments.

The one thing the site should get a visitor to do:

Contact the store or visit to buy instruments, sign up for lessons, or ask about services.

## Section map
| Tidepool section | Becomes | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Welcome section | Introduce Union Music and what it offers. |
| Marquee | Removed | It is not needed. |
| Gallery | Instrument categories | Show the different instruments and equipment. |
| About | About Union Music | Explain the store and its history. |
| Events | Services section | Show lessons, repairs, and rentals. |
| Contact | Contact and hours section | Help customers find and contact the store. |

## Brand
Colors: Dark blue, black, and white.

Fonts: Oswald and Open Sans.

Why these fit the business:

Dark blue and black give the website a clean music-store look, while white makes the information easy to read.
