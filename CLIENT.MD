# Client: Roomi's Barber Shop

Current online presence: https://roomis-barber-shop.business.site/

## Audit
Problems with their current presence (at least 3):
1. The website looks old and basic
2. The information is spread across different websites
3. The website could have better pictures and a more organized layout

## Audience and goal
Who their customers are:

People in Worcester looking for haircuts, beard trims, and shaves

The one thing the site should get a visitor to do:

Contact the barber shop to schedule an appointment.

## Section map
| Tidepool section | Becomes | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | Welcome section | Introduce the barber shop. |
| Marquee | Removed | It is not needed. |
| Gallery | Services section | Show the services offered. |
| About | About section | Tell visitors about the shop. |
| Events | Hours section | Show when the shop is open. |
| Contact | Contact section | Let customers contact the shop. |

## Brand
Colors: Black, white, and gold.

Fonts: Oswald and Open Sans.

Why these fit the business:

Black and gold give the website a barber shop look, and white makes it easier to read.
